Heritage

Our History

1990
1990

The Beginning

Calicut Watch House began as a Regional Stockist of Titan Watches, serving North Kerala — Palakkad, Thrissur, Malappuram, Kozhikode, Wayanad, Kannur, and Kasargod.

2017

The Showroom

A multi-brand watch and clock showroom was established at SM Street, Court Road — a milestone that marked the brand's evolution into retail and professional service.

Today

Eranhipalam

Now located at Wayanad Road, Eranhipalam, Kozhikode. Serving customers across the region with trust, expertise, and the same founding commitment to quality.
