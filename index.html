Our History

1990
1990

The Beginning

Calicut Watch House began as a Regional Stockist of Titan Watches, serving North Kerala — Palakkad, Thrissur, Malappuram, Kozhikode, Wayanad, Kannur, and Kasargod.

2017

The Showroom

A multi-brand watch and clock showroom was established at SM Street, Court Road — a milestone that marked the brand's evolution into retail and professional service.

Today

Eranhipalam

Now located at Wayanad Road, Eranhipalam, Kozhikode – 673020. Serving customers across the region with trust, expertise, and the same founding commitment to quality.

The People

Our Team

Since 1990, our management and staff have navigated every market shift — building over 36 years of trust, customer relationships, and deep expertise across sales and service.

01

Sales Team

Our sales professionals guide every customer from inquiry to purchase — backed by a strong supplier network sourcing unique products including custom-built oversized clocks for building tops, roadside junctions, and landmark installations.

02

Service Technicians

A dedicated crew of specialists — watch polishers, glass fitters, watchmakers, striking-clock technicians, welders, drillers, and small-parts craftsmen. We also have experienced hands for Swiss movement repair and service, with guaranteed workmanship.

03

Clock Specialists

Expert suppliers for double-sided export-quality station clocks, Wi-Fi clocks that sync with atomic precision, digital clocks, and specially crafted Azan clocks for masjids — serving institutions, public spaces, and places of worship.

Expertise

Our Services

Any type, any model, any brand — Indian or imported, latest or vintage. We take up the challenging works others won't.

01

Watch & Clock Repair

All brands, all eras. From everyday quartz to rare mechanical and vintage movements — handled with precision tools and trained hands. We accept any challenge.

02

Clock Restoration

Wall, station, pendulum, striking, digital, and Azan clocks — vintage or modern. Repairs others decline are our specialty.

03

Battery & Glass

Rapid battery replacement and crystal glass fitting. Same-day service for most models.

04

Nationwide Courier Service

Can't visit us? Send your watch by courier from anywhere in India. We repair and return safely via DTDC — trusted by customers across the country.

05

Swiss Movement Service

Experienced technicians for Swiss movement repair and servicing — a rare capability offered by very few shops in Kerala. Guaranteed workmanship.

06

Watch Strap Restoration NEW

We now restore old and rare watch straps — because some designs were discontinued long ago, leaving customers without a solution. Now they have one.

07

Watch & Storage Cases

Electronic watch cases and custom storage solutions — for customers who need that extra care for their timepieces.

08

Polishing & Finishing

Restore the original lustre of your watch case and bracelet — scratch removal, case polishing, and glass buffing, all handled by skilled hands.
